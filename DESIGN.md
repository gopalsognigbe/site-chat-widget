# Design System: Site Chat Widget + Fleuve Immobilier

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Every Answer Has an Address"**

This file records two systems that ship together and must stay separate. The **widget** (`widget/widget.js`, "the route") is the product: an assistant that lives in the corner of any client's website. Its signature is a route: each answer starts at an origin stop, and when the answer cites pages, a line runs from that stop down to filled, numbered source stops. The widget owns that shape and its motion; it borrows its colour from the client (`data-color`), takes a light or dark palette (`data-theme`), and can adopt the host's typeface (`data-font`, `data-font-src`). It renders in a Shadow DOM behind `:host{all:initial}`, so no host CSS reaches it, and none of the site's tokens below apply inside it.

The **demo site** (`demo_site/`, Fleuve Immobilier, 10 EN + 10 FR pages) is the first client: a fictional Montréal agency built to the category standard of luxury real estate. Warm paper, warm ink, one bronze accent, a night band to close. Libre Caslon Display carries headings, names and prices; Hanken Grotesk carries everything functional. Structure comes from hairline rules rather than boxes, photographs sit in 4px frames and drift slightly on scroll, actions are pills. It embeds the widget with `data-color="#8b6d47"` (its bronze), `data-theme="light"` and `data-font="Hanken Grotesk"`, which is the reference configuration in the review screenshots.

Density differs by design: the widget is messaging-app dense (15px text, 20px between messages, 392px panel), the site is editorial and airy (17px body at 1.65, sections 4.5–8rem apart, a 1240px measure).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Widget: one runtime brand colour; every brand tone and its contrast is derived, never hand-picked.
- Widget: the route (origin stop, brand line, numbered stops renumbered 1..k) is the identity, in light and dark.
- Widget: the visitor speaks in ink, the assistant speaks on the page with no bubble; brand belongs to the route.
- Site: paper, ink, one bronze, a night close; serif for names and figures, grotesk for function.
- Site: hairline-ruled lists instead of card grids; 4px photo frames; pill actions.
- Site: content is fully visible without JavaScript; GSAP/Lenis motion is an enhancement and is skipped under reduced motion.

## Colors

### Widget: Primary (runtime brand family)
The frontmatter shows the fallback brand (`#1e5aa8`) and its derived values on the light palette; at runtime every brand-family token is recomputed from `data-color`.
- **Client Brand** (`widget-brand`): `data-color`, normalised to 6-digit hex. Fills the launcher, the header mark, the origin stop, the numbered source stops, the send button, and a citation in its linked state.
- **On-Brand** (`widget-on-brand`, alternate `widget-on-brand-dark`): white when the brand reaches 4.5:1 against white, otherwise near-black `#111111`. The only text colour allowed on a brand fill.
- **Brand Ink** (`widget-brand-ink`): the brand used as text or line on the panel surface (route line, citation numerals and ring, chip arrows, focus outlines, caret, focused composer ring, typing dots). Starts as the brand and is mixed 8% per step away from the surface (toward black on light, toward white on dark), up to 20 steps, until it reaches 4.5:1.
- **Brand Wash** (`widget-brand-wash`): brand 12% into the surface. The halo around the origin stop, the halo of a linked source stop, text selection.
- **Brand Mist** (`widget-brand-mist`): brand 6% into the surface. Background of a hovered or linked source row and a hovered chip; the outer halo of the typing pulse.

### Widget: Neutral (light palette, `data-theme="light"`)
- **Panel White** (`widget-surface`): panel, chips, citations, composer, retry.
- **Warm Tone** (`widget-tone`): the header band and the disabled send button.
- **Warm Ink** (`widget-ink`): answer text, chip text, source page names.
- **Warm Grey** (`widget-muted`): subtitle, Sources label, source section suffix, placeholder, list markers, footer note, idle header icons.
- **Hairline** (`widget-line`): header bottom rule, chip ring at rest, scrollbar thumb.
- **Control Stone** (`widget-control`): the resting composer ring and disabled send icon (non-text boundary at 3:1).
- **Visitor Voice** (`widget-voice` on `widget-on-voice`): the visitor's bubble, ink-filled with white text.

### Widget: Neutral (dark palette, `data-theme="dark"`)
The dark palette mirrors every light role one-to-one: `widget-dark-surface` (panel), `widget-dark-tone` (header), `widget-dark-ink` (text), `widget-dark-muted`, `widget-dark-line`, `widget-dark-control`, and the visitor bubble inverts to `widget-dark-voice` with `widget-dark-on-voice` text. `color-scheme` follows the theme.

### Widget: Tertiary
- **Error Brick** (`widget-danger` on `widget-danger-soft`; `widget-dark-danger` on `widget-dark-danger-soft` in dark): error bubbles, the error origin stop and its halo, the retry pill. The only non-brand hue, and it appears only when something failed.

### Site: Primary
- **Bronze** (`site-bronze`): the bronze "Search listings" button, list markers, step numerals, the FAQ plus, the focus outline, text selection.
- **Deep Bronze** (`site-bronze-deep`): hover state of every ink action (primary pills, nav CTA, search submit), link hover inside content, the neighbourhood name on listings.
- **Bronze Mist** (`site-bronze-mist`): hover fill of white pills over the hero or night, table row hover.

### Site: Neutral
- **Paper** (`site-paper`): page background and the solid header (94% paper with a 10px backdrop blur).
- **White** (`site-white`): the search bar, boxed cards, filter status, text on night and on photographs.
- **Warm Ink** (`site-ink`): headings, body text, primary pill fills, the active segment.
- **Soft Ink** (`site-ink-soft`): leads, nav links, secondary prose.
- **Stone** (`site-stone`): meta lines, field labels, table heads, captions, listing IDs.
- **Hairline** (`site-line`) and **Strong Hairline** (`site-line-strong`): every rule; strong for the top rule of a ruled list, ghost pill rings and link underlines.
- **Night** (`site-night`), **Night Soft** (`site-night-soft`), **Night Text** (`site-night-text`), **Night Muted** (`site-night-muted`): the closing band and footer, the hero backdrop before its photo loads, footer rules and the fiction note.

### Named Rules
**The Borrowed Colour Rule.** The widget ships no hue of its own. Every chromatic pixel outside the error state derives from `data-color`, and contrast is computed (on-brand by contrast test, brand ink by stepping away from the surface), so any client colour stays AA in both themes.

**The Route Owns the Brand Rule.** Inside the panel, brand appears only on the route (origin stop, line, numbered stops, citations), the header mark, the send button and focus. The visitor's bubble is ink, the header is tone, the answer has no fill.

**The One Bronze Rule.** On the site, bronze marks the primary search action and small markers (bullets, step numbers, the FAQ plus, focus). Everything else is ink, paper and stone; ink actions warm to deep bronze only on hover.

## Typography

**Widget font:** the system UI stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`), with `data-font` prepended when the host supplies one; `data-font-src` additionally registers a woff2 `@font-face` (weights 400–700, swap). The demo passes `Hanken Grotesk`.
**Site display font:** Libre Caslon Display (with Iowan Old Style, Georgia), self-hosted woff2, latin + latin-ext subsets, weight 400 only.
**Site body font:** Hanken Grotesk (with system-ui), self-hosted variable woff2 400–700, latin + latin-ext.

**Character:** The widget adopts whatever the host reads in and lets size and weight do the work. The site pairs a high-contrast display serif, used large and at a single weight, with a clear, slightly warm grotesk.

### Hierarchy: Widget
- **Title** (600, 15px, 1.25, -0.005em): client name in the header; single line with ellipsis.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.55): answers, welcome, visitor bubbles. Bold is 650; paragraphs 8px apart, list items 4px, lists indented 18px with muted markers.
- **Input** (400, 16px, 1.4): composer, auto-growing to 120px.
- **Chip** (400, 14px, 1.35): suggested questions.
- **Source** (500 page name, 400 section, 13.5px, 1.3): source rows.
- **Meta** (400, 12.5px, 1.3): header subtitle; the footer note is 12px with 0.01em tracking; the retry label is 13px/600.
- **Label** (600, 11px, 0.08em, uppercase): the "Sources" label above the stops.
- **Numeral** (700, 10.5px, line-height 1, tabular): citation and stop numbers; the header mark's initial is 15px/600.

### Hierarchy: Site
- **Display** (400, clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem), 1.02, -0.015em): page h1, balanced; the hero headline caps at 11ch, inner page titles at 16ch. Large statement h2s (home statement up to 4.6rem, closing band up to 4.2rem) reuse this voice at 1.02.
- **Headline** (400, clamp(2rem, 3.6vw, 3.1rem), 1.08, -0.015em): section h2.
- **Index** (400 serif, clamp(1.5rem, 2.4vw, 2.1rem), -0.01em): guide index rows; the same serif at 1.25–1.6rem names neighbourhoods, services (1.45rem), footer heads (1.35rem), table captions (1.4rem) and the wordmark (1.6rem, line-height .95, "Immobilier" at .62em).
- **Price** (400 serif, 1.55rem, 1.1, lining figures): listing prices; step numerals are the same serif at 2rem in bronze.
- **Title** (600 sans, 1.15rem, 1.3, -0.005em): h3, listing names (1.2rem), FAQ questions (1.08rem).
- **Lead** (400, clamp(1.15rem, 1.6vw, 1.4rem), 1.55): intro paragraphs at 60ch; the hero lede is clamp(1.05rem, 1.4vw, 1.25rem) at 46ch.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.65): prose capped at 70ch; strong is 600.
- **Nav / Button** (500 at .9rem / 600 at .98rem): navigation and pills.
- **Meta** (400, .88rem): listing facts joined by a hairline-coloured middle dot, captions, hero foot line.
- **Label** (600, .72–.78rem, 0.08em, uppercase): search field labels, badges, table heads, definition terms, neighbourhood fact names; listing IDs use the same size and tracking in tabular figures without uppercase.

### Named Rules
**The Single-Weight Serif Rule.** Libre Caslon Display is used at 400 only, for headings, names, prices and numerals. Interface text, labels and buttons are always Hanken Grotesk.

**The Sixteen-Pixel Input Rule.** The widget composer stays at 16px while body is 15px, so mobile Safari never zooms on focus.

**The Tight Punctuation Rule.** Widget text passes through a filter that binds `? ! : ; »` to the preceding word (and `«` to the next) with a no-break space, so French punctuation never starts a line.

## Layout

**Widget.** Two fixed objects 22px from the bottom-right corner: a 58px launcher and, 94px from the bottom, a 392px panel at `min(660px, 100vh − 118px)`. The panel is a column: tone header with a 1px rule, scrolling log, composer, footer note. The log pads 22px top, 18px right, 10px bottom, 16px left, with 20px between messages. Visitor messages align right at up to 88% width; answers stretch full width with a 32px left indent that holds the route. The composer sits 14px from the panel sides. Under 480px the panel becomes the screen (`100dvh`, no radius or border), the launcher hides while open, header and footer pad into safe-area insets, messages widen to 92%, page scroll locks and Tab is trapped.

**Site.** One centred container, `min(100% − 2 × gutter, 1240px)`, gutter `clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 3rem)`. An 84px sticky header (absolute over the home hero, fixed and solid once the hero scrolls past). Sections open with `clamp(4.5rem, 10vw, 8rem)` of space; a section head puts the h2 left and an arrow link right on a shared baseline. Grids are auto-fit (300px or 440px minimums) with a `clamp(1.25rem, 2.5vw, 2rem)` gap. The home hero is full-bleed, up to `min(100svh, 980px)`, content anchored bottom-left. Lists are ruled rows: a strong top hairline, a hairline under each row, ~1.35–1.6rem row padding. Neighbourhood articles split 5:7 with a sticky 4:5 photo. Breakpoints step down at 1080px (nav to menu), 1000px (neighbourhood strip becomes a snap scroller), 900px and 860px (two-column blocks and the search bar stack), 760px (hero scrim goes vertical, image bands to 4:3) and 640px (section heads stack).

## Elevation & Depth

**Widget.** Two floating objects, a flat interior. The launcher casts a brand-tinted shadow and the panel a soft neutral one with a hairline border at 8% ink. Inside the panel there are no drop shadows: outlines are inset rings, and the route's stops wear spread-only halo rings in Brand Wash.

**Site.** Almost flat. Depth comes from photographs, scrims and the night band. The one cast shadow sits under the hero search bar, lifting it off the photo; the solid header uses translucency and blur instead of a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Widget launcher** (`0 10px 28px -8px color-mix(in srgb, brand 70%, transparent), 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.14)`), hover deepens to 75% / 14px / 32px with a 2px rise.
- **Widget panel** (`0 30px 70px -20px rgba(0,0,0,.34), 0 6px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`): the open panel.
- **Widget stop halo** (`0 0 0 4px brand-wash`): origin stop always; linked source stop, with a 1.08 scale.
- **Widget rings** (`inset 0 0 0 1px line` chip, `1px control` composer, `2px brand-ink` focused composer, `1.5px brand-ink` citation).
- **Site search lift** (`0 24px 60px -24px rgba(0,0,0,.55)`): the hero search bar only.
- **Site rings** (`inset 0 0 0 1px line-strong`, `ink` on hover): ghost pills and the guide index arrow circle; `inset 0 -2px 0 bronze` marks the focused search field.

### Named Rules
**The Two Floats Rule.** In the widget only the launcher and the panel cast shadows; interior separation is tone, rings and halos.

**The Inset Ring Rule.** Outlines in both systems are inset box-shadows, not borders, so a state change never shifts geometry.

**The Scrim Rule.** Site gradients exist only as dark scrims (`rgba(10,10,10,…)`) under text on photographs: left-to-right plus a bottom fade on the hero (vertical below 760px), a bottom fade on neighbourhood tiles.

## Shapes

**Widget.** Round and soft. Everything that is a stop or a button is a circle: launcher, header mark, icon buttons, send, origin stop (16px), source stops (20px), typing dots. The composer is a 24px-radius capsule, the panel 20px, the visitor bubble 18px with a 6px bottom-right tail, the error bubble 14px, chips 12px, source rows 10px, citations and retry full pills. The route line is a 2px rounded rule. Icons are authored SVG on a 24px grid, 1.8 stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`: 24px in the launcher, 19px in the header, 16px in chips and retry, 20px elsewhere.

**Site.** Quiet and nearly square. Every photograph frame takes a 4px radius with overflow hidden and an oversized image (112–118% tall) so it can drift; the search bar shell is 6px and its inner segments and submit 4px; actions (primary, ghost, nav CTA) and badges are full pills; the guide index arrow is a 44px circle. Ruled lines are 1px; the FAQ toggle is a 14px plus of 1.5px bronze bars that rotates to a cross. Photo aspect ratios: 3:2 listings, 21:9 bands and page headers, 3:4.4 neighbourhood tiles, 4:5 sticky article photos.

## Components

### Widget: Launcher
A labelled brand pill that becomes its own close button, so visitors notice the assistant at once.
- **Shape (closed):** 58px-high pill, brand fill, on-brand 24px icon then a 15px/600 label ("Ask our assistant" / "Posez votre question", overridable with `data-label` and `data-label-fr`), padding 0 24px 0 17px.
- **Shape (open):** the label collapses (max-width and margin to 0, 400ms) and the pill becomes a 58px circle holding the close icon.
- **Intro:** on the first page of a visit only, the label slides out 500ms after load (900ms); later pages show it at rest. Reduced motion shows it at rest.
- **States:** hover rises 2px with the deeper shadow; active scales to .97; focus uses the brand-ink outline offset 4px.
- **Icon morph:** chat and close share one cell; on open, chat rotates 90° and shrinks to .6 while fading, close rotates in from −90° (400ms transform, 200ms opacity).

### Widget: Panel and Header
- **Panel:** surface fill, 20px radius, panel shadow, 8% ink hairline. Opens from the launcher: from `translateY(14px) scale(.94)` and opacity 0, transform origin on the launcher, over 420ms transform and 220ms opacity. A panel restored open after navigation does not animate.
- **Header:** tone band with a bottom hairline. A 36px brand mark holding the client name's initial, the title over a muted subtitle ("Answers from this website"), and two 38px round icon buttons (new conversation, close) in muted grey that wash to 7% ink and turn ink on hover.

### Widget: Messages

- **Error:** soft brick bubble with brick text, a brick origin stop, and a retry pill on the latest failure. An interrupted previous answer renders the same way.

### Widget: The Route (signature)
Every answer starts somewhere and ends at its pages.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** derive every widget brand tone from `data-color` at runtime: on-brand by a 4.5:1 test against white, brand ink stepped 8% away from the surface until 4.5:1, wash and mist by `color-mix()` into the surface.
- **Do** give every assistant message an origin stop, and draw the line and filled numbered stops only when the answer has sources, renumbered 1..k with citations using the same numbers.
- **Do** keep the visitor bubble in the palette's voice colour (ink on light, inverted on dark) and the header in tone.
- **Do** keep widget light and dark palettes role-for-role identical so `data-theme` swaps values, never structure.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion` in both systems: the widget zeroes every transition and animation and shows the panel without transform; the site skips `motion.js` entirely and kills CSS transitions.
- **Do** keep the site's content visible without JavaScript; motion and the fixed header are gated on the `.js` class.
- **Do** reserve bronze on the site for the search action and small markers, and send ink actions to Deep Bronze on hover.
- **Do** structure site lists with hairline rules (strong top rule, hairline per row) and frame every photograph at 4px with an oversized image.
- **Do** use Libre Caslon Display at 400 for headings, names, prices and numerals, Hanken Grotesk for everything else, both self-hosted with latin and latin-ext subsets.

### Don't:
- **Don't** hard-code a hue into the widget or reuse the site's bronze inside it; the demo's bronze arrives only through `data-color`.
- **Don't** put raw brand on the panel as text or line, or any text on brand other than on-brand.
- **Don't** add drop shadows inside the widget panel; only the launcher and panel float, interior stops use spread-only halos.
- **Don't** drop the widget composer below 16px.
- **Don't** let host styles reach the widget; everything stays in the Shadow DOM behind `:host{all:initial}`.
- **Don't** build site sections as grids of boxed cards with icons; the boxed card is limited to short fact groups.
- **Don't** put an eyebrow or kicker above a site heading; uppercase tracked micro-type is for field labels, badges, table heads, definition terms and the widget's Sources label.
- **Don't** use gradients for decoration on the site; gradients are scrims for text on photographs.
- **Don't** add a second accent or let bronze fill large areas; night is for the closing band, footer and hero backdrop only.
