Visits

How do I book a visit?

Call us, send an email or use the contact page, and quote the listing ID (for example LI-003). We usually confirm a time within 24 hours on working days. Visits are possible Monday to Saturday, and some evenings on request.

Are visits free?

Yes. Visits are always free for buyers and tenants, whether in person or by live video. Tenants never pay an agency fee, and buyers usually do not either, because our fee is paid by the seller or the landlord.

Can I visit a property before I move to Montréal?

Yes. We organize live video visits, show you the street and the surroundings, and send extra photos and videos on request. Many clients relocating from another province or another country start this way, then visit in person before signing if possible.

Fees and payments

What are your fees?

In short: no fee for tenants and, in most cases, for buyers; 4% or 5% of the sale price for sellers; one month of rent for landlords on a 12-month lease; and 6% to 12% of the rent for property management. GST and QST are added. The full table is on our fees page.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer or wire (including international transfers), certified cheque, and Interac e-Transfer for smaller amounts. We do not accept cash above $500. For a purchase, the deposit and the price go through a trust account, never in cash to the seller.

What is the welcome tax, and is it included in the price?

The "welcome tax" is the common name for the transfer duties that a buyer pays to the municipality after buying a property in Québec. It is calculated in brackets based on the price, and Montréal has its own brackets for higher-priced properties. It is not included in our listed prices, and neither are notary fees. Your notary can estimate both before you sign.

Buying

Can foreigners buy property in Canada?

Canada has a temporary federal prohibition on the purchase of residential property by many non-Canadians, with exceptions (for example, permanent residents are not affected, and some temporary residents may qualify under conditions). This measure has been extended in the past and could change again. Before making an offer, confirm your situation with a notary or a lawyer. This is general information, not legal advice.

What is a certificate of location?

A certificate of location (certificat de localisation) is a document prepared by a Québec land surveyor. It describes the property, its boundaries and buildings, and shows whether it complies with titles, municipal by-laws and other rules. Sellers are usually asked to provide a recent one, and lenders and notaries rely on it.

How do you check that a property is safe to buy?

There are two kinds of checks. Legal: the notary verifies the title, hypothecs and rights in the Land Register of Québec and reviews the certificate of location. Physical: a building inspector inspects the property before your conditions expire. For condos, you also review the syndicate documents and the contingency fund.

How long does it take to buy a property?

Usually 30 to 60 days between the acceptance of your promise to purchase and the signing at the notary, and 2 to 4 months in total from the first visit. See the buying guide for the full timeline.

How much down payment do I need?

It depends on the price and your lender. In Canada, if your down payment is less than 20% of the price, mortgage default insurance is generally required. Get a pre-approval from a bank or a mortgage broker before visiting. We can introduce you to mortgage professionals, but we do not give financial advice.

Renting and management

How much do I pay before moving into a rental?

For a residential lease in Québec, generally only the first month's rent. Landlords generally cannot require a security deposit, and we never charge tenants an agency fee. For example, for a 4½ at $2,100 per month, you pay $2,100 before moving in. Details are on the renting page.

What do 3½ and 4½ mean?

It is the Québec way of counting rooms, where the bathroom counts as a half. A 3½ has one bedroom, a 4½ has two bedrooms and a 5½ has three bedrooms, each with a living room and a kitchen. A 1½ or 2½ is a studio.

I live outside Québec. Can you manage my property in Montréal?

Yes. Our management packages (6%, 8% or 12% of the rent, plus taxes) cover rent collection, transfers to your bank account in Canada or abroad, maintenance, inspections, renewal notices and finding new tenants. You receive a monthly statement by email. See fees.

Do you work in English?

Yes. Our whole team works in French and English, from the first call to the signing. In Québec, notarial deeds can usually be drafted in French or English at the parties' choice; confirm with your notary. Some official forms are mainly in French, and we help you understand them.
