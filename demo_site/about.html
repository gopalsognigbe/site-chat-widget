Our story

Fleuve Immobilier was founded in 2014 by Isabelle Tremblay, after ten years helping companies relocate employees to Montréal. She saw that newcomers and people from other provinces often struggled with local rules: the role of the notary, the welcome tax, Québec leases, or what a 4½ actually is.

The name comes from the St. Lawrence River, called "le fleuve Saint-Laurent" in French, which surrounds the island of Montréal.

The agency started with two people in a small office on the Plateau. Today our team of 9 has helped more than 900 clients buy, rent, sell or manage property, and we manage around 120 apartments, condos and offices on behalf of owners.

What we believe in

  • Documents first. We review the documents of every property before listing it, and we always recommend an inspection before buying.
  • Clear prices. Our fees are published, and agreed in writing before we start. Tenants never pay agency fees.
  • Two languages. Every client can work with us in French or English, from the first call to the signing.
  • Safe payments. Deposits and purchase funds go through a trust account, always with a receipt.

Where we work

Our office is on the Plateau, and we cover the island of Montréal, with most of our activity in Le Plateau-Mont-Royal, Griffintown, Verdun, Notre-Dame-de-Grâce and Old Montréal. On request, we can also help with properties in Laval and on the South Shore.
