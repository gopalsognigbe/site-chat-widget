Visits can be arranged outside office hours on request, including weekday evenings. For urgent issues in a property we manage (water leak, no heating in winter, break-in), tenants can call the emergency line 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What to include in your message

  • To book a visit: the listing ID (for example LI-002), your preferred days and times, and whether you want an in-person or video visit.
  • For a valuation: the address or neighborhood, the type of property (condo, plex, house), the approximate living area and the year of construction.
  • To find a rental: your monthly budget, the size (3½, 4½, 5½), furnished or not, and your move-in date.
  • For property management: the address and type of property, the number of units, and whether they are currently rented.

Getting here

Our office is on the Plateau, about 5 minutes' walk from a station on the orange metro line. Paid street parking is available nearby, and there are bike racks in front of the building. Please call ahead if you want to meet a specific team member.

Before you contact us, you may find your answer in the FAQ or on our fees page. You can also browse listings, neighborhoods, and our guides to buying, renting and selling, or read about us.
