Real estate in Montréal, explained clearly.

Since 2014, our team has guided more than 900 clients through property projects on the island of Montréal. Whether you are moving to Canada, relocating for work, or investing in a plex, we explain each step in plain English or French.

We work mainly in Le Plateau-Mont-Royal, Griffintown, Verdun, Notre-Dame-de-Grâce (NDG) and Old Montréal. Every property we list has had its documents reviewed, and every purchase is completed in front of a notary, as is standard practice in Québec.

  • BuyingSearch, visits, promise to purchase, inspection and coordination with the notary until you get your keys.
  • RentingFurnished and unfurnished apartments, condos and offices, with a lease on the official Québec form and a move-in inventory.
  • SellingFree valuation, professional photos and 3D tour, buyer screening and support until the deed is signed.
  • Property managementRent collection, maintenance and tenant relations for owners, including owners living outside Québec.
