Examples

  • Buying a townhouse at $789,000: no agency fee for the buyer. You pay the notary fees and the welcome tax.
  • Selling a cottage at $1,149,000 with an exclusive contract: commission 4% = $45,960, plus GST and QST ($6,882.51) = $52,842.51.
  • Renting an unfurnished 4½ at $2,100 per month: the tenant pays no agency fee, only the first month's rent. The landlord pays $2,100 plus taxes for the tenant search.
  • Management of a 5½ rented at $2,400 per month, Essential package: $144 per month plus taxes.

Payment methods: bank transfer or wire, certified cheque, or Interac e-Transfer for smaller amounts. Deposits for purchases are held in trust by the notary or our trust account. We do not accept cash above $500. Every payment receives a written receipt.
