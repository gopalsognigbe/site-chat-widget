Selling at the right price, to the right buyer.

A realistic price, qualified buyers and a smooth signing at the notary. Here is how we sell condos, plexes and houses in Montréal.

General information, not legal advice. The documents and obligations of a seller depend on the property and its legal situation. Your notary will confirm what applies to you.

How we sell your property

  1. First meeting and visitWe visit your property, listen to your goals (price, timing, whether you are buying another property at the same time) and explain the process. This visit is free and without commitment.
  2. ValuationWithin 5 working days, you receive a written valuation report with a recommended price range (see below).
  3. Documents checkWe review your documents with you before marketing starts: your deed of purchase, certificate of location, tax bills and, for a condo, the syndicate documents. If your certificate of location is out of date, we explain how to order a new one from a land surveyor.
  4. Brokerage contract and seller's declarationsYou sign a brokerage contract that sets the asking price, our commission and the duration, usually 3 to 6 months. You also complete the seller's declarations, a form describing the known condition of the property.
  5. MarketingProfessional photos, a 3D virtual tour, a detailed listing on our website and the main real estate portals, promotion to our database of buyers, and a sign on the property if you agree.
  6. Visits and buyer screeningWe handle all visits and open houses, and check that buyers are pre-approved before presenting their offers.
  7. Promises to purchaseWe present every written promise to purchase with our advice on price, conditions and dates. You decide which one to accept, refuse or counter.
  8. Conditions and notaryWe follow the buyer's inspection and financing until all conditions are met, then coordinate with the notary until the deed of sale is signed and you receive the funds.

Valuation: how we set the price

A realistic price is the most important factor for a quick sale. Our valuation takes into account:

  • recent sale prices of similar properties in the same neighborhood;
  • the living area in square feet, the lot size and the number of bedrooms and bathrooms;
  • the condition of the building, its age and recent renovations (roof, windows, electrical, plumbing);
  • for a condo: the condo fees, the health of the contingency fund and the building's amenities;
  • for a plex: the current rents, the leases in place and the potential return for an investor;
  • the municipal assessment and the current market: how many buyers are looking for this type of property.

The valuation is free if you sell with us. If you only need a valuation report (for a family, divorce or estate file), the fee is $350 plus taxes.

Documents to prepare as a seller

  • Two pieces of ID.
  • Your deed of purchase (the notary can find a copy if needed).
  • A recent certificate of location, prepared by a land surveyor.
  • Your latest municipal and school tax bills, and your mortgage statement.
  • Recent heating and electricity costs, and invoices for major renovations.
  • For a condo: the declaration of co-ownership, recent minutes of meetings, the budget and the contingency fund study.
  • For a plex: the current leases and the list of rents.

Selling from outside Québec

Some of our sellers live in another province or abroad. We handle visits and negotiations for you, and the notary can explain how to sign remotely, for example through a power of attorney.
